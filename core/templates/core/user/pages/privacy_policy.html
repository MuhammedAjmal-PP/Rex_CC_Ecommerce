Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you create an account, place an order, or contact us. This includes your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment details (handled securely via Razorpay).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process orders, personalise your shopping experience, send transactional emails, and improve our services. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Cookies

We use session cookies for login management and analytics cookies (via anonymous identifiers) to improve site performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function correctly.

4. Data Security

All data transmissions are SSL-encrypted. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing. Payment information is never stored on our servers — it is processed directly via Razorpay.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Contact us at support@rexcc.com to exercise your rights.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Significant changes will be notified via email or a notification on our website.
